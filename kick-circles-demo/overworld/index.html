KICK Circles — one-page pitch

01 · WHAT IT IS

Recommendations that build communities, not queues.

KICK Circles turns co-watching signals into private invitations to living communities. The overworld is the funnel: broad biomes lead to Circle villages, which lead to intimate rooms where people chat, contribute, grow status and return together. With a world atlas loaded, the top 100 streamers, 96 taxonomy places and 2,500+ semantic links become literal geography.

OverworldRegion gateCreator pavilionCircle islandRoom / caveTribe identity
02 · DATA SIGNALS

Proof of affinity.

Repeat viewing, active watch time, chat hours, clip engagement and creator collaborations form a co-watching graph. No single click enrolls anyone.

03 · CIRCLE DETECTION

Communities with proof of life.

Community detection promotes clusters only after minimum size, creator diversity and sustained activity. Short spikes become pop-ups. Identity is expressed through game, style and lifespan.

04 · QUALIFICATION

Behaviour recommends. It never enrols.

Invites remain private until accepted, appear only after steady interest across multiple creators, and always include “Why am I seeing this?” plus “Not for me.”

05 · CIRCLE SCORE

Bond is legible and cools honestly.

Affinity, Participation, Consistency and Contribution combine into named ranks. Cooling dims the plaza flame and wilts your garden, while varied participation restores it.

Primary: weekly return rate for users who follow at least one Circle. Secondary: pulse-notification opens, mission completion and Bond-maintenance sessions.
